# Prenders

Prenders, by [Comprenders](https://comprenders.com), is the app for your teams of
Claude Code agents.

Build teams of Claude Code agents for each project, for example development, marketing,
business opportunities and customer communication, and put them on any computer of your
network.

From any of your devices, your phone too, you see all your projects and chats, answer your
agents, steer the work and set up your computers.

Your network is private to you: your devices connect only to each other, and no outside
server can read what they send.

## Download

Download the app from the [newest release](https://github.com/comprenders/prenders/releases/latest).
Each release has:

- a `.dmg` for each Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel);
- a `-setup.exe` and an `.msi` for Windows;
- the `.apk` of the Android app.
